The opening of the novel immediately positions the reader to feel uneasy. The narrator describes the town as "a place where nothing ever happened", this suggests the town is hiding something. The dull setting makes the later events more shocking, the contrast forces the reader to question appearances. The author also uses short sentences to build tension, its effective because the reader feels the narrators fear early. Overall the opening succeeds because it makes the reader suspicious of the town, the calm surface is clearly a warning sign and the reader is positioned to distrust it from the start.
