The lighthouse had been empty for years. Nothing. Just wind and the slow rot of the stairs.

Mara pushed the door open and called out "is anyone there" The echo answered before the house did. She climbed anyway, her torch shaking in her hand.

"Your not supposed to be here" said a voice from the dark.

She froze. The beam of her torch caught a boys face, pale as chalk. "I live here" he said "I always have."

Mara didnt run. That was the strange thing. She just stood there, listening to the waves count themselves against the rocks.
