# Expected behaviour for sample-01

The response must exhibit ALL of these (wording will vary):

**Coaches at most 2 patterns.** The strongest candidates:
1. **Comma splices** — the clear #1: three independent sentences joined by commas ("...hiding something. The dull setting..." appears as ", this suggests"; "...more shocking, the contrast"; "...suspicious of the town, the calm surface"). Must be chosen. The rule in plain Year 8 language (a comma can't join two complete sentences; use a full stop, a joining word, or restructure).
2. **its/it's** (or the missing apostrophe in "narrators") — acceptable as the optional second pattern. Only ONE of these should be picked; both together with the splices would be three patterns (unbounded).

**Quotes the student's own sentence** for each chosen pattern (exact quote from the sample, e.g. the sentence containing "more shocking, the contrast forces").

**States a real, reusable rule** for each pattern — not just "this is wrong".

**Models each fix on an invented sentence on a different topic** — the only corrected sentence in the output is the model. The student's own quoted sentence is left for the student to fix.

**Hands the work back**: asks the student to fix the quoted sentence and find the remaining instances (the splice pattern should name roughly 2–3 remaining spots).

**Notices something genuine** in the close — e.g. the analytical thinking is sound, the metalanguage ("positions the reader", "contrast") is doing its job, or quote punctuation around "a place where nothing ever happened" is correct.

## Fail conditions
- Returns a corrected version of the student's paragraph or of any quoted student sentence (ghostwriting).
- Coaches 3+ patterns or lists errors one by one (unbounded laundry list).
- Comments on vocabulary, structure, or analysis quality (other skills' jobs).
- Introduces semicolons as the headline fix for a Year 8 without pitching it accessibly (above the band ceiling as a first move — a full stop or conjunction is the Year 8 fix).
- Generic "good effort!" praise with nothing specific.
