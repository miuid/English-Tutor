Both Macbeth and Ozymandias show that power does not last. In Macbeth, Shakespeare shows Macbeth asking the stars to hide his "black and deep desires", which shows he knows his ambition is wrong. The dark imagery makes the audience feel his ambition is dangerous. Shelley shows the statue of Ozymandias ruined in the desert, and the words "Look on my Works, ye Mighty, and despair!" show that his power is gone. This shows that both writers think power is bad and it makes you feel that powerful people always fall.
