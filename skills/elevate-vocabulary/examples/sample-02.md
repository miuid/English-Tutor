Shakespeare shows that ambition corrupts Macbeth. He shows Macbeth asking the stars to hide his "black and deep desires", which shows he is kind of ashamed of what he wants. The dark imagery makes you feel his ambition is really dangerous. It is a really good quote and it shows how ambition changes him a lot.
