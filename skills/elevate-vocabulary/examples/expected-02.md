# Expected behaviour for sample-02

The response must exhibit ALL of these (wording will vary), calibrated to the **year-9-10 tiers**:

**Picks the top 2–3 highest-value moves**, not every weak word. The strongest candidates:
1. **"shows"** — used 4 times; a bare analytical verb. Upgrades: *conveys / reveals / suggests / portrays / foregrounds*, varied to avoid repetition. This is the #1 pick (repetition + analytical payoff).
2. **Register breaks: "kind of", "really", "makes you feel"** — at Year 9–10 register upgrades rank as high as word swaps: *somewhat / arguably / profoundly dangerous*, and "makes you feel" → *positions the reader to feel / evokes*. (Register transition is the band's signature move.)
3. **"really good"** — vague judgement. Upgrades: *effective / compelling / powerful* — and note it should say *why*.

**Each upgrade has a genuine reason** (shade of meaning / precision / register), not a bare synonym list. E.g. "suggests" = implied vs "reveals" = made clear; "you" → "the reader" = formal academic register.

**Introduces band-appropriate Tier 3 metalanguage** (e.g. "positions the reader", "representation") with a short explanation on first use — not Year 12-level jargon.

**Returns the rewrite to the student** — presents options and asks the student to choose the ones fitting their meaning and rewrite the sentences themselves.

**Register**: within the Year 9–10 tier ceiling; any Tier 3 word is explained.

## Fail conditions
- Rewrites the paragraph wholesale (ghostwriting).
- Lists 6+ word swaps (unbounded).
- Treats the informal register ("kind of", "you", "really") as acceptable at this band.
- Suggests a word that changes the student's meaning without noting it.
- Comments on paragraph structure or analysis depth (out of scope — that's `check-structure`).
- Drops an unexplained senior-level word on a Year 9.
