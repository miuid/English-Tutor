# Expected behaviour for sample-03

The response must exhibit ALL of these (wording will vary), calibrated to the **year-11-12 tiers**:

**Picks the top 2–3 highest-value moves**, not every weak word. The strongest candidates:
1. **"shows"** — used 5 times; a bare analytical verb. Upgrades: *constructs / represents / foregrounds / exposes*, varied to avoid repetition. This is the #1 pick (repetition + analytical payoff).
2. **Register breaks: "makes you feel", "you"** — at senior level register upgrades rank as high as word swaps: "makes you feel" → *positions the audience to feel / invites the reader to take up*; "you" → *the audience / the reader*. (The IA2 audience is a public one; the EA audience is educated — never "you".)
3. **"bad" / "is gone"** — vague judgement. Upgrades: *power as corrosive / self-defeating; authority rendered hollow* — and note it should name the *values* at stake, not just the direction.

**Each upgrade has a genuine reason** (shade of meaning / precision / register), not a bare synonym list. E.g. "constructs" names the writer's deliberate choice; "represents" fits the official ISMG lexis.

**Introduces senior Tier 3 metalanguage** — the official syllabus terms (*representation*, *positioning / positions the audience*, *cultural assumptions / values*, *aesthetic features*, *stylistic devices*) with a short explanation on first use — these are the words the ISMG descriptors are written in.

**Returns the rewrite to the student** — presents options and asks the student to choose the ones fitting their meaning and rewrite the sentences themselves.

**Register**: within the senior tier ceiling; any Tier 3 word is explained on first use.

## Fail conditions
- Rewrites the paragraph wholesale (ghostwriting).
- Lists 6+ word swaps (unbounded).
- Treats the informal register ("you", "makes you feel") as acceptable at this band.
- Suggests a word that changes the student's meaning without noting it.
- Comments on paragraph structure, synthesis or analysis depth (out of scope — that's `check-structure`).
- Uses the official terms (*representation*, *positioning*) without explaining them on first use.
