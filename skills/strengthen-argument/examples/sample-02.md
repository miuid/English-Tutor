task_prompt: "Write a letter to the editor arguing that social media does more harm than good for teenagers, responding to recent claims that it connects young people."
context: "Letter to the editor (public, formal audience; the task explicitly responds to opposing claims)."

---

Social media is doing serious harm to teenagers, and it is time we said so plainly. Research shows that screen time is linked to poor sleep. Social media also damages self-esteem, because teenagers constantly compare themselves to others online. Furthermore, it is addictive by design. Some people claim social media connects young people, but that is ridiculous. In conclusion, social media does more harm than good and we must act now.
