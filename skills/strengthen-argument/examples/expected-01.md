# Expected behaviour for sample-01

The response must exhibit ALL of these (wording will vary):

**Traces the chain accurately.** Contention is present and clear ("should not be compulsory"); reasons exist but are asserted ("unfair", "expensive") with no elaboration; the "everyone knows they are uncomfortable" line is decorative evidence. Must NOT claim the contention or reasons are missing.

**Focuses on exactly ONE broken link.** The correct pick by the priority rule is **reason without elaboration** (break type 2) — e.g. develop "unfair" into *why* it is unfair and why classmates should care. Picking decorative evidence instead is acceptable only if reason-without-elaboration is explicitly named as "next time"; flagging several breaks at once is a fail.

**Names what works specifically** — e.g. the position is clear from the first line and restated at the end. No empty praise.

**Models the repair on a DIFFERENT topic** (e.g. homework, phones in class), showing a reason developed into a because/which-means move in 1–3 sentences. Must NOT rewrite or complete the student's uniform sentences.

**Hands the work back**: asks the student to pick one of their own reasons and write the "why it holds / why we should care" themselves; a sentence stem ("Uniforms are unfair because…, which means…") is appropriate scaffolding at Year 8.

**Ends with a concrete "I can…" criterion**, e.g. "I can explain why my reason matters, not just state it."

**Does NOT demand a rebuttal** — this speech to a friendly class audience does not invite opposition.

**Register**: encouraging Year 8 voice; treats the asserted reasons as a sound start, not a failure.

## Fail conditions
- Rewrites the student's paragraph or supplies the missing elaboration for the uniform argument (ghostwriting).
- Flags two or more broken links as the focus (unbounded).
- Claims there is no contention or no reasons (misread of the chain).
- Comments on word choice ("bad idea" → stronger verbs) or paragraph structure — out of lane.
- Invents a rebuttal requirement the task doesn't have.
