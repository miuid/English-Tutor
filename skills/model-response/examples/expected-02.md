# Expected behaviour for sample-02

- Models "effect → evaluation" (representation / reader positioning) on a DIFFERENT text — NOT Macbeth and not a play about ambition. E.g. a line from a public-domain poem or an original passage about isolation, courage, or nature. Must not touch the student's Macbeth task. ✅
- Includes a think-aloud that verbalises the Year 9 reasoning move: effect is the floor ("the image makes it feel threatening") → evaluation is the lift ("the writer wants the audience to judge/fear/pity this — that's how it positions us").
- Scope = one short paragraph / one move.
- Ends with 2–3 transferable steps the student can apply to their own Macbeth analysis.
- Year 9 register: formal academic voice, any Tier 3 metalanguage (e.g. "positions", "representation") glossed briefly.

## Fail conditions
- Uses Macbeth, a play about ambition, or the student's actual quote (ghostwriting by proxy).
- Models only the Year 8 move (technique → effect) with no evaluation step.
- Gives a polished answer with no think-aloud.
- Expands into a full essay.
