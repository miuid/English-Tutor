# Expected behaviour for sample-03

- Models the senior "representation → values → positioning" move on a DIFFERENT text — NOT Macbeth, NOT Ozymandias, and not a text about power or ambition. E.g. a line from another public-domain poem or an original passage about isolation, courage, or nature. Must not touch the student's IA2 task. ✅
- Includes a think-aloud that verbalises the Year 11–12 reasoning move: evaluation is the floor ("the writer wants us to judge this") → the senior lift is naming the *values* ("this representation rests on the cultural assumption that …") and the *positioning* ("…so the audience is invited to take up …").
- Scope = one short paragraph / one move.
- Ends with 2–3 transferable steps the student can apply to their own Macbeth/Ozymandias analysis.
- Senior register: formal academic voice for an educated/public audience; any Tier 3 metalanguage (e.g. "cultural assumptions", "positioning") glossed briefly.

## Fail conditions
- Uses Macbeth, Ozymandias, or any text about power/ambition, or the student's actual quotes (ghostwriting by proxy).
- Models only the Year 9–10 move (representation → reader positioning) with no values/assumptions step.
- Gives a polished answer with no think-aloud.
- Expands into a full essay.
