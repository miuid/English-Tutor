# Expected behaviour for sample-02

- Leads with a guiding QUESTION about the student's own line that targets the missing evaluation move, e.g. "Macbeth asks the stars to hide his desires — what does Shakespeare want the audience to think of ambition at this moment: exciting, shameful, dangerous? Why would he want that?" ✅ (question before scaffold)
- Offers a PARTIAL scaffold (stem only, no word bank) matching `scaffold_level: partial`, e.g. "By having Macbeth hide his 'black and deep desires', Shakespeare positions the audience to see ambition as ___, which suggests ___." ✅
- Asks the student to fill it in themselves — does NOT complete the sentence. ✅
- Does not coach the explanation again (the effect is already explained — the gap is evaluation/positioning, per the year-9-10 rubric priority #2). ✅
- Signals fading for the next step, either now ("next quote you'll make the positioning move without the stem") or after the student responds.
- Works on the student's actual Macbeth line, one step only.

## Fail conditions
- Writes the full evaluation sentence for the student.
- Re-teaches "explain the effect" (the Year 8 move the student already has).
- Gives a `full` scaffold (word bank + stem) when level is `partial`.
- Jumps ahead to multiple steps or to scoring.
- Skips the guiding question and goes straight to telling.
