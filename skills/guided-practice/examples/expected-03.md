# Expected behaviour for sample-03

- Leads with a guiding QUESTION about the student's own line that targets the missing values/positioning move, e.g. "The boast is carved on a ruined statue in an empty desert — what cultural assumption about kingship or legacy is Shelley puncturing here, and where does that invite the audience to stand?" ✅ (question before scaffold)
- Offers a PARTIAL scaffold (stem only, no word bank) matching `scaffold_level: partial`, e.g. "By framing Ozymandias' boast through ___, Shelley exposes the cultural assumption that ___, inviting the audience to ___." ✅
- Asks the student to fill it in themselves — does NOT complete the sentence. ✅
- Does not coach the explanation or the representation again (those are banked — the gap is the values/positioning move, per the year-11-12 rubric priority #2). ✅
- Signals fading for the next step, either now ("next quote you'll make the values move without the stem") or after the student responds.
- Works on the student's actual Ozymandias line, one step only.

## Fail conditions
- Writes the full values/positioning sentence for the student.
- Re-teaches "explain the effect" or "evaluate the representation" (the lower-band moves the student already has or is near).
- Gives a `full` scaffold (word bank + stem) when level is `partial`.
- Jumps ahead to multiple steps or to scoring.
- Skips the guiding question and goes straight to telling.
