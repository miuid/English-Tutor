# QCAA task specifications (Year 9–10 analytical)

Source: reaserch.md (QCAA Year 8/9 assessment techniques & conditions; approved assessment formats). **Provenance note (Q-001):** Year 9 rows quote reaserch.md; Year 10 rows are derived from the same Years 9–10 assessment-conditions band and are provisional until official QCAA sources are imported.

## Year 9–10 conditions

| Technique | Mode | Length | Time (assessment mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short response | written / spoken / multimodal | written 100–300 words per item (≤800 total); spoken ~1–2 min | within exam block |
| Extended response | written / spoken / multimodal | written **600–800 words**; spoken 3–5 min; multimodal 4–6 min | over an extended period |
| Examination | written, supervised | short 100–300/item; extended 600–800 | up to **90 min + 10 min planning** |

Scope at this band: critical analysis, adapting text structures, and establishing voice (reaserch.md — Extended Response Scope, Year 9); precise vocabulary and editing for structural accuracy (Short Response Scope, Year 9).

## Analytical task formats (Year 9–10)

Critical literary essay · comparative essay · website critique · preface anthology. Focus: evaluate how representations of people, places, events and concepts reflect historical, social or cultural contexts, and how texts position readers (reaserch.md — Analytical formats, Year 9 row).

## What changes from Year 8

- Word count floor rises 400–600 → **600–800**; exam time 70 → **90 min** (+10 planning both).
- The task focus moves from *explaining how features shape meaning* to *evaluating representations, contexts and positioning*.
- Comparative formats appear (two texts), so task briefs may ask for integrated comparison, not two sequential halves.

## Copyright-safe stimulus rules

- Use public-domain texts (pre-1955 AU / classic poetry & prose; Shakespeare is public domain and a genuine Year 9 set-text author) or generate an original passage.
- Never paste in a school's copyrighted set text; refer to it by name and have the student supply their own copy if analysing it. Common Year 9 copyrighted set texts (e.g. *Jasper Jones*, *The Book Thief*) must never be reproduced as stimulus.
