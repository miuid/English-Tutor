# QCAA task specifications (Year 9–10 persuasive)

Source: reaserch.md (QCAA Year 8/9 assessment techniques & conditions; approved assessment formats). **Provenance note (Q-001):** Year 9 rows quote reaserch.md; Year 10 rows are derived from the same Years 9–10 assessment-conditions band and are provisional until official QCAA sources are imported.

## Year 9–10 conditions

| Technique | Mode | Length | Time (assessment mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short response | written / spoken / multimodal | written 100–300 words per item (≤800 total); spoken ~1–2 min | within exam block |
| Extended response | written / spoken / multimodal | written **600–800 words**; spoken **3–5 min**; multimodal **4–6 min** | over an extended period |
| Examination | written, supervised | short 100–300/item; extended 600–800 | up to **90 min + 10 min planning** |

Scope at this band: critical analysis, adapting text structures, and establishing voice (reaserch.md — Extended Response Scope, Year 9).

## Persuasive task formats (Year 9–10)

Feature articles · letters to the editor · campaign pitches · formal reviews. Focus: expressing viewpoints on **social themes** (e.g. celebrity culture, technology) using **rhetorical strategies and evidence** (reaserch.md — Persuasive formats, Year 9 row).

## What changes from Year 8

- Word count floor rises 400–600 → **600–800**; spoken 2–4 → **3–5 min**; exam time 70 → **90 min** (+10 planning both).
- Formats shift from speeches/vlogs to **public written formats** — the register is formal-public by default, and the format's conventions (a letter's salutation and sign-off, a feature article's headline and hook) are part of the task.
- The task focus moves from *adapting voice to an audience* to *sustaining a substantiated viewpoint through deliberate rhetoric*.

## Copyright-safe stimulus rules

- Use original/generated scenarios and social themes — persuasive tasks need an issue, not a set text.
- Never paste in copyrighted opinion pieces, editorials, or campaign material as stimulus; generate an original passage in the format instead.
- Public-domain material (e.g. historical speeches out of copyright) may be used as stimulus where relevant.
