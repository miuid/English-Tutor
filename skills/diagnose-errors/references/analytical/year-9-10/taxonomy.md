# Error taxonomy + routing (Year 9–10 analytical)

Used by `diagnose-errors`. Categories are ordered by leverage — fix higher categories first. Same five categories as Year 8; the band difference is what counts as "sound" inside each one (anchored to reaserch.md — Year 9 marking criteria and analytical-essay objectives).

| # | Category | What it looks like at Year 9–10 | Routes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Argument / point** | No thesis or only a descriptive claim; retells plot; doesn't engage with representation or context; doesn't answer the question. | `check-structure` (Point/thesis) |
| 2 | **Analysis / evidence** | Explains a technique's effect but never evaluates it (no representation/context/positioning move); quote-dumping; asserts without proof. | `check-structure` (E1/E2) |
| 3 | **Structure / cohesion** | Elements out of order; paragraphs don't advance the thesis; weak links; conclusion repeats rather than synthesises. | `check-structure` (L / essay-level) |
| 4 | **Language / vocabulary** | Informal register ("kind of", "really", "you"); flat/repeated analytical verbs; missing band-appropriate metalanguage. | `elevate-vocabulary` |
| 5 | **Mechanics** | Grammar, punctuation, spelling errors (only once higher levels are sound). | (future) `fix-mechanics` |

## Priority rule
Choose the primary issue as the **lowest-numbered category rated "major"**. Rationale: a strong vocabulary can't rescue a response with no argument, so fix the argument first. Mechanics is last — don't polish sentences that have no point.

## Band calibration
- An explanation-only paragraph is **sound at Year 8** but only **C-level at Year 9–10** — do not flag "explains the effect" as a strength gap at this band; flag the *missing evaluation* instead (category 2).
- Informal register is a **major** category-4 issue at this band (Year 9–10 assessment expects a formal academic register; reaserch.md — Objectives: Technical Writing Mechanics), whereas at Year 8 it is usually minor.

## Strong-response case
If nothing is "major" and at most one thing is "minor", route to `give-feedback` with a *stretch* target (e.g. sharpen the thesis from sound to discriminating, deepen one analysis into evaluation) rather than inventing a fault.
