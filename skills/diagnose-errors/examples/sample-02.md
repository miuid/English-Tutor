task_prompt: "How does Shakespeare present the corrupting effect of ambition in Macbeth?"

---

Shakespeare shows that ambition corrupts Macbeth. In his soliloquy Macbeth says "Stars, hide your fires; let not light see my black and deep desires." This shows that Macbeth knows his desire to be king is wrong and he wants to hide it. The dark imagery makes the audience feel that his ambition is dangerous. This is a really good quote that shows how ambition changes him.
