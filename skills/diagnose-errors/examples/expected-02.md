# Expected behaviour for sample-02

Ratings (wording may vary, direction must match), calibrated to the **Year 9–10** taxonomy:
- Argument/point: **minor** — a claim exists ("ambition corrupts Macbeth") and answers the question.
- Analysis/evidence: **major** — at this band, explaining the effect ("dark imagery makes it feel dangerous") is the floor, not the ceiling: no evaluation of representation or reader positioning.
- Structure/cohesion: **minor** — basic flow exists, no strong link back to a thesis.
- Language/vocabulary: **minor** — "shows" ×3, "really good"; a register/word-choice issue but not the primary gap.
- Mechanics: **ok**.

Primary issue = **Analysis / evidence** (lowest-numbered "major"; the missing evaluation move, per year-9-10 priority #2). ✅
Route to = **check-structure** (E1 depth: extend effect → representation/positioning). ✅

- Diagnosis ONLY — no coaching, no rewriting, no score. ✅
- Exactly one primary issue and one route.
- Must NOT rate analysis as sound just because the effect is explained — that calibration is Year 8, not Year 9–10.

## Fail conditions
- Coaches or rewrites (that's check-structure's job).
- Routes to multiple skills at once.
- Picks language/vocabulary as primary while analysis is major (violates priority order).
- Judges the paragraph as sound analysis (applies Year 8 calibration at Year 9).
