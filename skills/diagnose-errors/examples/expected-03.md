# Expected behaviour for sample-03

Ratings (wording may vary, direction must match), calibrated to the **Year 11–12** taxonomy (IA2 + EA):
- Argument/point: **minor** — a claim exists ("power does not last") and spans both texts, but it is a thematic statement rather than a critical perspective on the representation.
- Analysis/evidence: **major** — at this band, explaining the effect ("dark imagery makes it feel dangerous") is below the floor: no analysis of the cultural assumptions, values or beliefs underpinning the representation of power, and no audience-positioning move (ISMG Knowledge application "superficial/adequate" territory).
- Structure/cohesion: **minor** — basic flow exists, but the two texts are treated as sequential halves (no synthesis); noted, not the primary gap.
- Language/vocabulary: **minor** — "shows" ×5, "makes you feel"; a register/word-choice issue but not the primary gap.
- Mechanics: **ok**.

Primary issue = **Analysis / evidence** (lowest-numbered "major"; the missing values/positioning move, per year-11-12 priority). ✅
Route to = **check-structure** (E1 depth: extend representation → cultural assumptions/values → positioning). ✅

- Diagnosis ONLY — no coaching, no rewriting, no score. ✅
- Exactly one primary issue and one route.
- Must NOT rate analysis as sound just because effects are explained and two texts are named — that calibration is Year 9–10, not senior.

## Fail conditions
- Coaches or rewrites (that's check-structure's job).
- Routes to multiple skills at once.
- Picks language/vocabulary as primary while analysis is major (violates priority order).
- Picks structure/cohesion as primary over analysis (sequential halves matter, but the missing values/positioning analysis caps the mark harder).
- Judges the response as sound analysis (applies a lower-band calibration at Year 12).
