Jack followed the track into the bush because he wanted to find the waterfall his brother always talked about. The track disappeared and he realised he was lost. He was very scared. Then he heard a noise in the dark and he was terrified. It was only a wallaby. He was so relieved. Eventually he saw the lights of a farmhouse and knocked on the door, and the farmer drove him home.
