# Expected behaviour for sample-01

The response must exhibit ALL of these (wording will vary):

**2–3 warm-up items, traceable to the digest.** The strongest targets, in priority order:
1. **Analysis at D** (weakest criterion) — an item reactivating the analysis move, e.g. recalling what "explaining the effect of a technique" means, or applying it in one line ("name one effect a short sentence can create").
2. **Recently coached `elevate-vocabulary`, 3 days ago** — the spacing target: recall or apply the coached vocabulary move (e.g. "name two precise verbs that could replace a flat one like *said*", or one-line show-an-upgrade).
3. Acceptable optional third: the PEEL structure coached via `check-structure`, or a fundamental today's war-poetry task needs (e.g. what counts as evidence in an analytical paragraph).

**No invented history.** Every item must trace to the digest above — no reference to sessions, criteria, or coaching the digest does not mention (e.g. no imaginative writing, no fix-mechanics, no made-up past scores).

**Items are retrieval, not teaching.** Each item is a question or one-line task answerable in a sentence or two — no rules explained, no worked examples, no hints that give the answer away inside the `## Warm-up` section.

**Self-check answers included** — one line per item under `## Check yourself`, matching the item count.

**Time and tone box respected.** 2–3 items only, completable in ~3 minutes; Year 8 plain metalanguage (technique, effect, evidence, PEEL); the opening line says why these items today without recapping grades (no "you got a D").

## Fail conditions

- More than 3 items, or a warm-up that teaches new content (unbounded / second lesson).
- An item that references history absent from the digest (invented memory).
- Quoting grades or levels back at the student as a scorecard ("last time you scored D in Analysis").
- Items requiring paragraph-length answers (that's the independent task's job).
- Answers revealed inside the `## Warm-up` section instead of `## Check yourself`.
- No self-check answers, or answers that don't match the items.
