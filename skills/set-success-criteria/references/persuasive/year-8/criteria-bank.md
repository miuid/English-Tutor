# "I can…" criteria bank (Year 8 persuasive)

Reusable, student-friendly phrasings mapped to QCAA Year 8 persuasive capabilities (reaserch.md — Year 8 marking criteria: advancing ideas with supporting evidence; adapting language and voice to formal or informal audiences). Adapt to the specific topic/audience — don't use verbatim if a more specific version fits.

## Position & argument
- I can state my position clearly in the introduction.
- I can give each paragraph one reason that supports my position.
- I can explain why my reason matters, not just state it.
- I can support my reasons with a specific example or fact.
- I can end with a call to action, not just a summary.

## Audience & voice
- I can match my language to my audience (formal or informal).
- I can speak directly to my audience when it helps my case ("you", "we").
- I can ask my audience a question they can't ignore.

## Vocabulary / language
- I can use strong persuasive words instead of "good" and "bad".
- I can choose how forceful my statements sound (might → should → must).
- I can avoid filler intensifiers like "very" and "really".

## Response-level (extended response / speech)
- I can keep one position across the whole response.
- I can hook my audience in the first lines.
- I can order my reasons so my strongest lands where it counts.

## Learning intention stems
- "We are learning to persuade an audience with developed reasons."
- "We are learning to adapt our voice to suit the audience."
- "We are learning to choose forceful, precise persuasive language."
