# "I can…" criteria bank (Year 11–12 analytical — IA2 + EA)

Reusable, student-friendly phrasings mapped to the official QCAA English 2025 v1.3 syllabus (© State of Queensland (QCAA) 2026, CC-BY 4.0; `research/official/english-2025-v1.3-syllabus.md`): IA2 assessment objectives 1–11 (p. 38), IA2 specifications (p. 39), EA objectives and specifications (p. 48). Adapt to the specific text/topic — don't use verbatim if a more specific version fits. EA variants are exam-mode analytical: same criteria, exam discipline.

## Thesis & critical perspective
- I can state a clear, arguable thesis that presents my interpretation of the text(s).
- I can sustain an informed and critical perspective across the whole response. (EA objective: communicate an informed and critical perspective, p. 48)
- I can give my analysis a purpose — a focused perspective (personal, philosophical, social, political or cultural) on the representation. (IA2 specifications, p. 39)
- In EA mode: I can answer the unseen question directly, not the question I hoped for.

## Analysis / Knowledge application (ISMG criterion 1)
- I can analyse how perspectives and representations of concepts, identities, times or places are constructed in the text(s). (IA2 objective 3)
- I can analyse the cultural assumptions, attitudes, values and beliefs that underpin the text. (IA2 objective 4)
- I can explain how the text invites its audience to take up a position. (IA2 objective 4)
- I can analyse the effects of aesthetic features and stylistic devices, not just name them. (IA2 objective 5)

## Evidence & synthesis / Organisation and development (ISMG criterion 2)
- I can select and synthesise subject matter so every quote genuinely supports my perspective. (IA2 objective 6)
- In IA2: I can connect the two texts in an integrated way — one representation, two constructions, not two mini-essays.
- I can organise and sequence my ideas so the argument builds, with cohesive devices that emphasise my ideas. (IA2 objectives 7–8)
- In IA2: I can use the conventions of my chosen genre (article, blog, essay or column) and maintain my role as a writer for a public audience. (IA2 objectives 1–2)

## Language / Textual features (ISMG criterion 3)
- I can make deliberate language choices for my purpose and audience. (IA2 objective 9)
- I can write in a formal register with accurate grammar for an educated/public audience. (IA2 objective 10; genre note p. 12)
- I can use senior analytical metalanguage precisely (e.g. *representation, positioning, cultural assumptions, aesthetic features, stylistic devices*).

## Learning intention stems
- "We are learning to analyse how representations are underpinned by cultural assumptions, values and beliefs."
- "We are learning to sustain an informed and critical perspective for a public audience."
- "We are learning to synthesise evidence from two connected texts into one argument."
- "We are learning to answer an unseen question with a discriminating thesis under exam conditions." (EA mode)
