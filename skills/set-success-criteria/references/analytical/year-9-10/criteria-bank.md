# "I can…" criteria bank (Year 9–10 analytical)

Reusable, student-friendly phrasings mapped to QCAA Year 9 analytical capabilities (reaserch.md — Year 9 marking criteria: considered analysis of representations, contexts, positioning; discriminating thesis; formal register). Adapt to the specific text/topic — don't use verbatim if a more specific version fits.

**Provenance note (Q-001):** Year 10 phrasings are derived from the Year 9 elaborations.

## Thesis & argument
- I can state a clear, arguable thesis that answers the question.
- I can make each paragraph's point advance my thesis.
- I can write a conclusion that pulls my argument together, not just repeats it.

## Analysis / evaluation
- I can explain how a technique creates an effect, not just quote it.
- I can analyse how the writer constructs a representation (of a person, place, event or idea).
- I can explain how the text positions the reader to think or feel.
- I can link a writer's choice to its context (historical, social or cultural).

## Evidence
- I can choose quotes that genuinely prove my point.
- I can embed a quote smoothly into my own sentence and analyse it.

## Vocabulary / register
- I can write in a formal academic register (no "you", "really", "kind of").
- I can use analytical metalanguage precisely (e.g. "positions", "representation", "motif").
- I can vary my analytical verbs instead of repeating "shows".

## Comparative tasks (Year 9–10 formats)
- I can compare two texts in an integrated way, not one after the other.
- I can explain a key similarity or difference in how the two texts position readers.

## Learning intention stems
- "We are learning to analyse how writers construct representations."
- "We are learning to evaluate how texts position readers."
- "We are learning to build an argument around a discriminating thesis."
- "We are learning to write in a formal academic register."
