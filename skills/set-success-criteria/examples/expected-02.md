# Expected behaviour for sample-02

- One learning intention in plain language, e.g. "Today we are learning to analyse how a writer's choices position the reader."
- 2–3 "I can…" criteria (Year 9), observable and self-checkable, drawn from the year-9-10 criteria bank, e.g.:
  - I can explain how the text positions the reader to think or feel.
  - I can analyse how the writer constructs a representation.
  - I can write in a formal academic register.
- Because `focus_skill` = analysis, at least one criterion is explicitly analytical (representation / positioning / evaluation) — not just "explain the effect" (that is the Year 8 floor). ✅
- Criteria trace to the Year 9 outcome (representations + positioning), not generic and not Year 8 explanation-only.
- Ends by telling the student they'll self-rate against these.

## Fail conditions
- More than 4 criteria, or vague criteria like "I can write a good paragraph".
- Criteria that would be equally at home in Year 8 (explanation-only, no analysis/evaluation move).
- Restates the outcome in curriculum jargon without translating it.
