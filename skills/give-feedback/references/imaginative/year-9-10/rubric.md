# QCAA A–E feedback rubric (Year 9–10 imaginative)

Based on QCAA five-point (A–E) standard elaborations for Year 9 (reaserch.md — Evaluative Standards & Marking Rubrics: Year 9 rows) read against the imaginative domain — "conveys meaning and perspectives through narrative structure" (reaserch.md — Core Exam & Assessment Domains). The NAPLAN narrative criteria (reaserch.md — The NAPLAN Writing Evaluation Scale) inform the criterion set below. Criterion-referenced: judge against the descriptor, not against other students.

**Provenance note (Q-001):** Year 10 expectations are derived from the Year 9 elaborations, not verbatim QCAA text.

## Levels (general shape)

- **A** — *purposeful creation* with strong substantiation; *purposeful selection and considered experimentation* with text structures, language features, and features of voice; the reader can feel the choices were deliberate.
- **B** — effective, controlled narrative with some genuine experimentation; minor lapses in control or tone.
- **C** — sound: a developed narrative using standard structures and language features; a competent linear arc with an indistinct voice sits here.
- **D** — partial: thin complication for the length; key moments told; experimentation attempted but uncontrolled.
- **E** — *sporadic* production; fragmented structures the reader cannot follow.

## Per-criterion descriptors

| Criterion | A/B | C | D/E |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structural control** (NAPLAN: Text Structure, Cohesion) | Structure is a deliberate choice — non-linear, multi-voice, or POV play that shapes meaning and stays followable | Conventional arc, competently handled | Fragmented or confusing without design |
| **Story & tension** (NAPLAN: Ideas) | Layered complication sustains the full length; key moments slowed and placed for impact | Clear complication developed and resolved | Complication too thin for the length; stakes flat |
| **Voice & tone** (NAPLAN: Audience) | Narrative voice crafted; tonal shifts signalled and controlled; POV changes deliberate (reaserch.md — Imaginative formats, Year 9 row) | Consistent voice; limited tonal range | Voice indistinct; POV drift; accidental tone shifts |
| **Character & setting** (NAPLAN: Character & Setting) | Character perspective is the lens of the piece; setting accrues meaning (motif/symbol) | Character and setting established and consistent | Thin or interchangeable |
| **Language & imagery** (NAPLAN: Vocabulary) | Precise, voice-fitted diction; imagery sustained into motif; figurative language chosen for effect | Clear, appropriate choices with some precise detail | Formulaic or clichéd; "very" doing the work |

## Mapping to the coaching skills

- Structural control / Story weak → next step routes to `check-structure` (structural control, key moments).
- Voice & tone / Showing weak → next step routes to `craft-voice`.
- Language & imagery weak → next step routes to `elevate-vocabulary` (tonal control + motif).

## The Year 9–10 → A+ move

At Year 8 the lever is building tension into one complication. At Year 9–10 that is the floor: the A move is **deliberate craft the reader can feel** — a voice, structure, or tonal shift that was clearly chosen, key moments shown, and an ending that re-frames the whole piece.
