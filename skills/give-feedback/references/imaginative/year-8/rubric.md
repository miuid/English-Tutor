# QCAA A–E feedback rubric (Year 8 imaginative)

Based on QCAA five-point (A–E) standard elaborations for Year 8 (reaserch.md — Evaluative Standards & Marking Rubrics: Year 8 rows) read against the imaginative domain — "conveys meaning and perspectives through narrative structure" (reaserch.md — Core Exam & Assessment Domains). The NAPLAN narrative criteria (reaserch.md — The NAPLAN Writing Evaluation Scale: Audience, Text Structure, Ideas, Character & Setting, Vocabulary, Cohesion, Paragraphing) inform the criterion set below. Criterion-referenced: judge against the descriptor, not against other students.

## Levels (general shape)

- **A** — *purposeful creation* of an imaginative text; purposeful variation of text structures and language features; considered features of voice that orient, engage, and affect the reader.
- **B** — effective narrative; controlled arc; mostly purposeful choices with minor lapses in tension or voice.
- **C** — sound: a clear complication developed and resolved; standard structures and language features.
- **D** — partial: events listed without built tension; key moments told; voice indistinct.
- **E** — basic *statements* of events; fragmented structures; sporadic language features.

## Per-criterion descriptors

| Criterion | A/B | C | D/E |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Story & tension** (NAPLAN: Text Structure, Ideas) | One clear complication, tension built scene by scene, key moment slowed down, resolution that costs something | A clear problem developed and resolved in order | Events listed; no real complication or an unearned ending |
| **Character & setting** (NAPLAN: Character & Setting) | Characters act consistently and feel specific; setting shown through concrete detail | Character and setting established and maintained | Characters interchangeable; setting absent or generic |
| **Showing & voice** (NAPLAN: Audience) | Key emotions and moments shown, not named; narrator voice deliberate | Some shown detail; voice mostly consistent | Emotions labelled; key moment summarised; voice indistinct |
| **Language & vocabulary** (NAPLAN: Vocabulary) | Precise verbs and concrete sensory detail; figurative language that earns its place | Clear, mostly appropriate word choices | Flat verbs; vague adjectives; "very" doing the work |
| **Structure & cohesion** (NAPLAN: Cohesion, Paragraphing) | Scene breaks at time/place shifts; opening hooks; ending lands | Functional beginning/middle/end; paragraphs mostly controlled | Scenes blur; abrupt or missing ending |

## Mapping to the coaching skills

- Story & tension / Structure weak → next step routes to `check-structure` (complication, rising tension, resolution).
- Showing & voice weak → next step routes to (planned ISS-008) `craft-voice`.
- Language & vocabulary weak → next step routes to `elevate-vocabulary` (precision + show-don't-tell).

## The Year 8 C→A lever

Most Year 8 narratives sit at C because events are listed at one flat level. The highest-leverage next step is almost always: **slow down the key moment and show it — one scene, concrete detail, stakes that rise — and make the ending cost something.**
