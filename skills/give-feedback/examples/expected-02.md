# Expected behaviour for sample-02

Per-criterion A–E under a `## Per-criterion levels` heading, named exactly as in the year-9-10 `rubric.md`, in the `- <criterion>: **<level>** — <note>` shape (direction must match; calibrated to Year 9–10, not Year 8):
- Understanding of text / ideas: **C** — sound interpretation of the representation (ambition as corrupting); not yet purposeful or substantiated.
- Analysis (how techniques create meaning): **C/D** — the effect is explained (dark imagery → danger), which is the Year 9 floor; no evaluation of representation or positioning. C if the sound explanation is credited, D if the missing evaluation dominates. Either is acceptable.
- Use of evidence: **C** — relevant, embedded quote, but under-analysed.
- Structure & cohesion: **C/D** — basic flow (point → quote → explanation) but no real link back to a thesis; C if that flow is credited, D if the missing link dominates. Either level is acceptable.
- Language & vocabulary: **C/D** — understandable but flat and repetitive ("shows" ×3, "really good"); the register is acceptable but undistinguished. D is acceptable here.
- (Formative mode: overall level de-emphasised, but roughly **C**.)

- **One specific strength**: e.g. "you explained the effect of the dark imagery, not just named it — that explanation move is exactly what strong analysis is built on." (specific, real — not "good job"). ✅
- **1–2 next steps, no more**:
  1. Extend the analysis from effect to evaluation — e.g. what does Shakespeare want the audience to think about ambition when Macbeth hides his "black and deep desires"? — *why: at Year 9 this is the move that lifts Analysis from C toward A/B (considered analysis of representation/positioning).* Tie to the standard. ✅
  2. (optional) Swap one register break ("really good") for an analytical judgement and say why.
- Each next step has a *why* tied to the standard descriptor. ✅
- **Metacognitive prompt**: asks the student to self-rate against the criteria and compare. ✅
- Formative register: leads with growth, not the letter. ✅
- Does NOT rewrite the paragraph; coaching on step 1 hands to `check-structure`.

## Fail conditions
- Awards B/A for Analysis on an explanation-only paragraph at Year 9 (Year 8 calibration).
- Bare grade with no improvement path.
- 3+ next steps.
- Rewrites the student's paragraph.
- Generic praise; harsh tone.
- Corrects vocabulary in detail here instead of routing (a brief mention is ok; deep coaching → `elevate-vocabulary`).
