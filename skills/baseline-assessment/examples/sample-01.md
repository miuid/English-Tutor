I think Harry Potter is a really memorable character. He is memorable because he is brave and he never gives up even when things are really hard. In the book he fights Voldemort lots of times even though he is just a kid and could easily die. This shows he is brave. J.K. Rowling uses good describing words to make him seem real, like the scar and the glasses, so everyone knows who he is. He also has friends like Ron and Hermione who help him, which shows friendship is important. Overall Harry Potter is memorable because he is brave and a good friend and lots of people like him.
