Maya found the letter under the floorboard on the last day in the old house. The removalists were already carrying boxes past her door, and Mum kept calling her name from the driveway. The paper was yellow and soft at the creases, addressed to "whoever lives here now" in handwriting that leaned hard to the right. She sat down on the bare boards and read it twice. It was from a girl called Priya who had lived in Maya's room twenty years ago, and it said the house kept things: every family left something behind, and if you knew where to look, it gave something back. Under the last line was a list of hiding spots, and the third one was the crack behind the wardrobe in Maya's own room. Maya checked it before she left. Inside was a small tin with a marble in it, green as a bottle. She took the marble, wrote her own letter, and slid it under the floorboard for the next kid.
